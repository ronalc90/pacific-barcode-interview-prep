# Pacific Barcode Interview Prep

STAR interview study guide for the Customer Success Representative role at Pacific Barcode.

Open the live page: https://ronalc90.github.io/pacific-barcode-interview-prep/

## Features
- Adaptive multiple-choice quiz: miss a question and you get a similar one
- 41 interview questions with sample STAR answers
- Practice mode to hide answers and test yourself
- Story bank, role-fit map, situational scenarios and industry vocabulary
- Preparation checklist with progress saved in the browser
